# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Cloudwatcher is for privacy-conscious web users who want to understand when sites and page resources are delivered through Cloudflare. While browsing, they need fast, legible detection information and, when needed, straightforward controls for warnings, ignore rules, activity, and CIDR ranges.

## Product Purpose

Cloudwatcher passively makes Cloudflare delivery visible without modifying web traffic. It pairs evidence-led detection with local controls so people can manage warnings and their retained activity confidently. Success is a user who feels informed and in control rather than interrupted or uncertain.

## Positioning

A local-only, passive Cloudflare detector with no telemetry or remote lookups.

## Brand Personality

Vigilant, direct, protective. The interface should communicate observed facts plainly, make the safe next action obvious, and respect the user's agency without manufacturing alarm.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboard styling: decorative metrics, bloated navigation, corporate admin-panel conventions, and visual treatment that distracts from detection evidence and controls.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with observed evidence, not speculation.
2. Keep control local and legible.
3. Make protective actions calm and reversible.
4. Respect browsing flow with concise, low-friction interactions.
5. Earn trust through accessible, consistent behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain the existing accessibility baseline: strong contrast, keyboard-operable controls, clear focus states, responsive layouts, semantic announcements for status and errors, and reduced-motion support.
